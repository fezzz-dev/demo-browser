AI Text Summarizer – Options

Follow the steps below to connect the extension to OpenAI.

1
Get your OpenAI API key

Go to platform.openai.com/account/api-keys , sign in, and click Create new secret key. Copy the key — it starts with sk- and is shown only once.

2
Paste your key below and click Save

Your key is stored only in your browser and sent exclusively to api.openai.com.

3
Use the extension

Select any text on a web page, right-click, and choose Summarize with AI. The summary will appear in the extension popup.
